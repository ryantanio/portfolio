Personal participation comes from Ryan's confirmation. Public sources establish product scope; they do not independently establish his individual tasks. Contribution text uses his supplied full-stack responsibilities and avoids attributing individual product features, leadership, metrics, or sole ownership. Exact framework assignments are not known for every project, so capability labels are used rather than invented per-project React/Node/AWS stacks.
